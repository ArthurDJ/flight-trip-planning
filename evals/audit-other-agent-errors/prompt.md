Recommendation: Option 1.
Option 1: Delta, Denver to Los Angeles one-way, $289.
Option 2: American, Denver to Los Angeles round trip via Phoenix, $410. Option 1 is $121 cheaper.
Option 3: United premium economy, $520 round trip. Premium economy is refundable, so this is the safe choice if plans change.
Phoenix stop on Option 2: departs Denver 10:00 (Mountain Time), arrives Phoenix 11:30, so the flight is 1 h 30 min; the connection to LAX leaves 12:00, and 30 minutes is enough.
